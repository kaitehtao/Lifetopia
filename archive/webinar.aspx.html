Product Descriptions

Lifetopia hosts a range of student housing solutions for online housing contracts, roommate matching and room selection, online housing self-service, core housing management and room condition reporting. Learn how you can utilize and integrate these services at your institution without breaking the bank. We will show you how our system functions and display all online features within these webinars.

Room Condition Reporting

Lifetopia's Room Inventory and Condition Reporting software allows any web enabled mobile device to track and record the condition and damage assessment of each room before a student moves in and after they check out.


Online Housing Contracts

Save trees and paper by converting hard copy formats of your housing contract to online digital format. This allows students to review and electronically sign their online housing agreements saving hours of paperwork.


Online Roommate Matching and Room Selection

Lifetopia Roommates provides a safe and secure student portal to find and select potential roommates. We offer Lifetopia Roommates as a stand-alone college / university branded website or fully integrated with Facebook.


Online Housing Self-Service

Lifetopia's Housing is easy to use and automated, allowing students to fill out their housing application online, pay for their housing deposit, renew their housing contracts, and be assigned a specific bed.


Core Housing Management

Lifetopia's Housing Management System is an affordable user-friendly platform that provides solutions for managing residence life without the headaches. The technology provides administrators the efficient and time saving capability to automate assignments, view and track student progress, view historical data, and take advantage of our powerful reporting tools.

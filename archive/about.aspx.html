Housing Management

Lifetopia's Housing Management System is an affordable user-friendly platform that provides solutions for managing residence life without the headaches. Our web-based capabilities enable students to help themselves with online roommate searches, housing applications, building and bed selections, and much more.

Off Campus Housing

Lifetopia's Off Campus Housing Solution (OffCampusListing.com) centralizes the housing department's off-campus housing inventory into a single online marketplace within the school's website. Built with our award winning roommate matching solution, students are able to securely view all available housing options and make their selections online. For students unfamiliar with the local area, detailed maps and safety zones can be designated. Our solution allows the housing department to set the criteria for approved housing, including listing rates, as well as provide administrative oversight.

FAQ's

Do we have to install anything on our servers or have our I.T. department involved?

Our system is fully hosted so you do not need to install anything on your servers and our implementation is an easy smooth process. Depending on your setup I.T. normally has minimal involvement in the setup process.
